# Fermah Product and TGE Roadmap

**Planning window:** Q4 2026 to Q2 2027  
**Target TGE:** Q1 2027  
**Launch principle:** Core products live on mainnet before TGE  
**Audience:** External GTM, launch, partnership, and community teams

Fermah is bringing one connected product stack to market. **Kernel** runs programmable workflows. **Strata** turns that execution layer into managed prediction-market infrastructure. **Ultramint** is the consumer product that proves the entire stack in public.

This roadmap concentrates the company plan into three quarters. It covers customer-facing product outcomes and GTM proof points. Pricing, licensing mechanics, legal work, compliance, and detailed engineering delivery are outside its scope.

## How the Fermah stack works

| 01 · Kernel | 02 · Strata | 03 · Ultramint |
|---|---|---|
| **Execute anything.** Kernel runs durable workflows for applications, agents, APIs, private workers, and smart contracts. | **Operate markets.** Strata packages market contracts, creation, indexing, relaying, and managed resolution into one platform. | **Prove the product.** Ultramint is Fermah's own market application, community hub, and showcase for the complete stack. |

**Kernel powers Strata. Strata powers Ultramint. Ultramint proves demand for both.**

## Current position Q4 2026

### Kernel · Pre-production platform

Kernel already has a substantial execution foundation:

- A Rust workflow library and broad catalogue of reusable execution nodes.
- Containerized execution and an existing on-chain workflow oracle.
- MCP-assisted workflow discovery, composition, and validation.
- A separate SaaS application with teams, projects, visual workflow creation, run history, encrypted team secrets, and Stripe or USDC billing.
- A shared job queue and dedicated worker service running end to end in development.

The Q4 challenge is product unification and production deployment. The library, worker, hosted API, SaaS, MCP surface, and oracle need to feel like entry points into one Kernel network rather than separate projects.

### Strata · Integrated testnet stack

Strata already provides the majority of the core prediction-market stack:

- LS-LMSR contracts deployed on Base Sepolia.
- Binary, categorical, scalar, and combined-market primitives.
- Market creation, buying, resolution, disputes, claims, and settlement.
- A chain indexer, market read model, and gasless transaction relayer.
- A managed resolution engine with durable workflows and human-review support.
- AI-assisted creation and structured market term sheets inside Ultramint.

The Q4 challenge is turning these internal services into a clear partner product: one API, reusable frontend components, managed resolution, and a supported way to connect customer-owned data sources.

### Ultramint · Private beta

Ultramint is already a feature-rich staging product on Base Sepolia and GKE:

- AI-assisted market creation and gasless market transactions.
- Automated resolution, disputes, finalization, and claims.
- Portfolio, discovery, profiles, comments, follows, circles, alerts, notifications, rewards, referrals, and leaderboards.
- Resolution support for crypto, sports, YouTube, TikTok, social, and AI-researched markets.
- Read-only API access and a hosted MCP service prepared behind a disabled launch gate.

The Q4 challenge is moving the complete experience to mainnet, closing the remaining money-flow features, and converting existing social and reward primitives into the ambassador programme.

## Q4 2026 Mainnet foundation

**Quarter objective:** Put the complete core product stack into production before TGE.

### Kernel

1. **Unify the product.** Present the workflow library, Docker worker, hosted API, SaaS, MCP, and on-chain oracle as access methods to one Kernel execution platform.
2. **Launch the hosted control plane.** Support authenticated workflow submission, scheduling, status, cancellation, retries, and result retrieval through the API and SaaS.
3. **Launch a production worker pool.** Run multiple workers against a durable shared queue with safe job recovery and capacity-based routing.
4. **Complete shared workflow secrets.** Let teams securely reuse credentials across hosted and private executions without exposing values through the product.
5. **Ship developer access.** Publish the production Rust crate, Docker worker image, workflow format, starter templates, and integration documentation.
6. **Connect crypto-native billing.** Activate USDC-funded hosted executions alongside conventional SaaS billing.
7. **Expand MCP from authoring to operations.** Add authenticated submit, schedule, status, cancel, and result tools.

**End-of-quarter customer outcome:** A developer can build a workflow once and execute it through code, the SaaS, a hosted endpoint, MCP, a Docker worker, or a smart contract.

### Strata

1. **Deploy the Base mainnet contracts.** Launch the supported production market set with Fermah-aligned protocol fee routing.
2. **Launch Strata API v1.** Expose markets, quotes, positions, creation, transaction status, resolution status, and claims through one documented partner API.
3. **Package the managed backend.** Present indexing, relaying, market creation, and resolution as one hosted service rather than separate internal systems.
4. **Extract the first frontend kit.** Release reusable market cards, term sheets, creation, trade, position, and claim components.
5. **Create a stable source-adapter boundary.** Make it straightforward for Fermah to connect partner databases and third-party APIs without redesigning the resolver.
6. **Prove a partner integration.** Complete one non-Ultramint branded or internal design-partner implementation.

**End-of-quarter customer outcome:** A partner can build its own frontend while Fermah provides the market contracts, data plane, transactions, and managed resolution.

### Ultramint

1. **Launch on Base mainnet.** Deliver the full create, fund, buy, resolve, claim, and withdraw journey.
2. **Activate Onramper.** Enable production Base USDC funding from the in-app deposit experience.
3. **Complete rewards settlement.** Move rewards from recorded obligations to user-visible payouts.
4. **Launch the production source catalogue.** Productize crypto, sports, news and research, YouTube, TikTok, and social market creation.
5. **Start the ambassador beta.** Use the existing sponsor, referral, rewards, profile, and leaderboard systems for the first campaign cohort.
6. **Prepare account entitlements.** Introduce the usage counters and feature limits that paid tiers will build on in Q1.

**End-of-quarter customer outcome:** Ultramint is a live mainnet product with real creation, funding, trading, resolution, payouts, and community participation before TGE.

## Q1 2027 TGE and public activation

**Quarter objective:** Turn the TGE into an activation event for a product ecosystem that is already live.

### Kernel

1. **Open public hosted access.** Broaden access to the Kernel API and SaaS with clear onboarding and ready-to-run templates.
2. **Launch public MCP access.** Let approved agents create, submit, schedule, and monitor workflows through hosted MCP.
3. **Launch customer-operated workers.** Allow design partners to run approved Kernel workers in their own environment for data residency.
4. **Introduce regional worker pools.** Route workloads by region, capability, and execution requirement.
5. **Launch the workflow template catalogue.** Package reusable automations for research, market resolution, monitoring, notifications, and data collection.

**TGE utility:** Developers and agents can immediately consume execution capacity and contribute workflows to a live network.

### Strata

1. **Launch partner workspaces.** Give customers API credentials, market policies, source configuration, usage visibility, and lifecycle webhooks.
2. **Release the complete integration kit.** Support embedded creation, trading, portfolio, resolution progress, and claims rather than display-only widgets.
3. **Launch white-label configuration.** Add customer branding, themes, product copy, approved market templates, and integration callbacks.
4. **Release the supported source-adapter kit.** Provide a documented contract and validation path for customer-owned databases and business systems.
5. **Onboard initial launch partners.** Move the first white-label or co-branded prediction-market products into public use.

**TGE utility:** Partners can launch branded market experiences while Fermah operates the complex infrastructure behind them.

### Ultramint

1. **Launch paid tiers.** Increase API usage, MCP access, market creation limits, active market limits, and advanced creator capabilities.
2. **Launch hosted MCP.** Give agents supported market discovery, portfolio, creation, and account-aware workflows.
3. **Introduce agent identities.** Let approved agents participate as recognizable users with profiles, activity, permissions, and limits.
4. **Launch the ambassador programme.** Connect in-app campaigns, referrals, rewards, social actions, and Discord tasks into one progression system.
5. **Expand TGE market programming.** Run market campaigns across crypto, ecosystem milestones, media, sports, creators, and community events.
6. **Launch creator progression.** Make higher creation capacity and advanced tools attainable through paid tiers, performance, and community status.

**TGE utility:** The community can participate as traders, creators, ambassadors, developers, partners, and agent operators.

## Q2 2027 Advanced products and scale

**Quarter objective:** Expand the post-TGE ecosystem into advanced markets, broader partner deployments, and customer-controlled infrastructure.

### Kernel

1. **General availability for private workers.** Support customer-operated worker groups with workload routing and data-residency controls.
2. **Expand distributed execution.** Add multi-region worker coordination and additional workload-allocation strategies for higher scale and resilience.
3. **Support longer-running workflows.** Add richer recurring schedules, durable pauses, resumable jobs, and advanced execution policies.
4. **Open the integration ecosystem.** Expand the node and workflow catalogue with partner-built connectors and templates.
5. **Add team execution analytics.** Show usage, latency, success, spend, and worker activity across hosted and private capacity.

**End-of-quarter customer outcome:** Kernel operates as a hybrid execution network spanning Fermah infrastructure and customer-controlled workers.

### Strata

1. **Release the white-label starter application.** Provide a complete branded foundation for companies launching their own market site.
2. **Expand beyond Base.** Add the next EVM deployment targets based on partner demand.
3. **Launch self-service source onboarding.** Let partners configure and validate approved adapters with less Fermah implementation work.
4. **Add partner operations dashboards.** Expose market activity, resolution progress, relayer status, source usage, and customer-level analytics.
5. **Expand market templates.** Add packaged experiences for media, enterprise forecasting, ticketing, community governance, and event platforms.

**End-of-quarter customer outcome:** A partner can move from concept to a branded, managed prediction-market product through a repeatable Strata launch path.

### Ultramint

1. **Launch perpetual markets beta.** Introduce a limited set of perpetual markets with purpose-built positions, margin, risk, and liquidation experiences.
2. **Enable bounded agent actions.** Let approved agents create markets and place policy-limited trades through delegated user permissions.
3. **Expand paid plans.** Add perps, higher agent limits, larger API quotas, advanced creation, and additional active-market capacity.
4. **Expand the content engine.** Add more news, sports, entertainment, social, and creator-driven market feeds.
5. **Scale community seasons.** Turn the ambassador launch into recurring campaigns with teams, quests, partner programmes, and visible progression.
6. **Launch agent performance surfaces.** Add agent portfolios, history, rankings, strategy labels, and transparent activity feeds.

**End-of-quarter customer outcome:** Ultramint expands from a prediction-market application into a multi-format market and agent-participation platform.

## Message architecture

### One company story

**Fermah is the programmable execution and market infrastructure behind applications, agents, and on-chain products.**

### Three product stories

- **Kernel:** Build a workflow once and run it through code, hosted infrastructure, private workers, agents, or smart contracts.
- **Strata:** Launch a prediction-market product without building contracts, indexing, relaying, or a resolution operation.
- **Ultramint:** Create, trade, and resolve markets in a social product designed for people, creators, communities, and agents.

### TGE story

**The TGE activates participation in a live product ecosystem; it does not precede the products.**

## Scope discipline

The Q4 launch should remain focused on the complete mainnet user journey and the minimum partner-ready platform. The following capabilities belong in Q1 or Q2 and should not delay the pre-TGE mainnet release:

- Broad autonomous agent trading.
- Perpetual markets.
- General-availability white-label deployment.
- Broad multichain coverage.
- An open third-party workflow and adapter ecosystem.
- Fully decentralized worker coordination.

This sequencing keeps the TGE narrative credible: **mainnet proof in Q4, ecosystem activation in Q1, and category expansion in Q2.**
